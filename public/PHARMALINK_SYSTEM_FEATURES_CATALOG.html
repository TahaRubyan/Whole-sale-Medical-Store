🏛️ 1. Multi-Tenant SaaS & Super-Admin Governance

  • Super-Admin Portal: Master authentication (rubyan/1234) for central software distributors.
  • Automated Tenant Provisioning: Generates unique tenant_id (e.g. TENANT-1002) and isolated tenant databases.
  • 30-Day Automated Subscription Tracking: Live calculation of monthly billing renewal dates with high-contrast ACTIVE vs MEMBERSHIP DUE status badges.
  • Subscription Payment Logging: One-click monthly fee payment extension (+30 days) with complete transaction audit timestamps.
  • Security Onboarding: Mandatory password reset on first tenant login.

🔐 2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Multi-User Governance

  • Admin / Operations Director: Full uninhibited access across inventory, ledgers, pricing overrides, supplier POs, FBR tax audits, and settings.
  • Cashier Role Isolation: Strictly limited to POS fast checkout and sales return processing. Completely blocked from sensitive customer debt ledgers, audit logs, supplier balances, and system settings.
  • Immutable Author Audit Trail: Every single sales order, purchase order, batch stock edit, and refund note records the creating operator's name and role (e.g., Hassan (Cashier) vs Dr. Idrees (Admin)).

⚡ 3. Wholesale Fast POS & fast Billing Engine

  • Multi-Field Instant Search: Real-time indexing across Brand Name, Generic Formula, Item Code, Barcode, and Rack Location.
  • Full Keyboard Navigation: Use ArrowDown and ArrowUp to traverse live medicine suggestions, Enter to insert into cart, and Esc to dismiss.
  • Packaging Unit Conversion: Instant switching between whole Box packaging and loose units (Tablets/Strips) with dynamic price conversions.
  • Interactive Cart Line Edit Modal: Click edit to modify Line Discount %, Adv Tax % (0.5% default), Sale Tax % (18% default), and Unit Price with live preview calculations.
  • Multi-Channel Payment Settlement: Supports Cash with live Change Due calculation, Bank Transfer, Cheque, and Credit Receivables.
  • Real-Time Atomic Stock Deduction: Reduces batch boxes and tablets immediately upon checkout and syncs regional distribution logs.

📄 4. A4 Commercial Dual-Page Invoice & Thermal Receipt Engine

  • Drug Act Form 2 Legal Layout: High-contrast black & white visual hierarchy optimized for laser/inkjet printing and photocopying.
  • Intelligent 10-Item Multi-Page Pagination: Item rows seamlessly flow to Page 2 only when Page 1 capacity is reached (no overlapping text).
  • Full Header & Footer on Every Page: Every page retains distributor licensing details, customer credentials, legal warranties, digital signature stamp, and Page n of m pagination.
  • Pakistani Rupees In Words: Auto-translates numeric totals to words (e.g., "Rupees Twenty-Seven Thousand Seven Hundred Five and 30/100 Only").
  • Section 23 Drugs Act & DRAP Warranty: Formally embedded statutory warranty text with Urdu notice disclaimer.
  • Digital Signature & Stamp Badge: High-resolution PNG digital signature badge rendering with dual issuer and receiver signature lines.
  • 80mm / 58mm Thermal Receipts: Compact thermal slip generation with QR code verification and barcode footer.

📦 5. Inventory, Batch Management & FEFO Expiry Radar

  • 20+ Pharmaceutical Wholesale Catalog: Pre-loaded catalog of high-demand medicines across tablets, capsules, syrups, and antibiotics.
  • Granular Batch Tracking: Tracks Batch Number, Expiry Date, Total Boxes, Total Tablets, Purchase Cost, Wholesale Rate, and Supplier Distributor.
  • First-Expiry-First-Out (FEFO) Automation: POS automatically recommends and dispatches earliest expiring active batches.
  • Expiry Radar Dashboard: Color-coded alerts for Critical (< 3 Months / Expired), Near Expiry (< 6 Months), and Safe (> 1 Year) inventory.
  • Prescription (Rx) Safety Guard: Interactive toggle to flag prescription drugs with doctor note warning modals during POS checkout.
  • Stock Override & Adjustments: Authorized admin physical stock audits, sample deductions, and batch extensions.

📊 6. Stock Movement & Regional Distribution Audit

  • Stock Velocity & Turnover Calculation: Inward receiving vs outward sales box tracking and turnover percentage metrics.
  • Regional Distribution Matrix: Breakdown of commercial deliveries across North, Central, and South distribution territories (Gujrat, Lahore, Gujranwala, Faisalabad).
  • Regional Delivery Manifest Generator: Printable delivery dispatch sheets with driver names, vehicle numbers, invoice stop orders, carton counts, and receiver signature blocks.
  • Stock Movement Audit Print Modal: Official printable audit report for stock movement verification.

🏛️ 7. FBR Official Sales Tax Audit & Lawyer Schedule (Annexure-C)

  • Standard 18% Sales Tax + 0.5% Advance Tax: Automatically computed and aggregated across line items and invoices.
  • Audit Preset Time Windows: Filter by Today, Past 7 Days, Past 30 Days, Custom Date Range, or Credit Debt.
  • Itemized Sales Tax Breakdown: Medicine Brand, Generic Formula, Regulatory Category, Total Boxes Sold, Net Taxable Sales Value, and 18% Sales Tax Collected.
  • Official Lawyer Report Print Modal: Printable audit document with Annexure-C reference, NTN/STRN credentials, total tax collected summary cards, legal declaration, and verified signature badge.

👥 8. Customer Ledger & Credit Receivables Engine

  • Customer Master Directory: Full records with NTN, STRN, credit limits, phone numbers, and physical delivery addresses.
  • Chronological Ledger Statement: Tracks invoice history with DSS tracking numbers, invoice totals, payments received, and remaining credit debt.
  • Debt Settlement Modal: Record partial payments or full settlements with Cash, Bank, or Cheque tags.
  • Printable Customer Statement: Formal A4 Statement of Account with custom date range filters, aging breakdown, and payment history.

🚚 9. Supplier Directory, Purchase Orders (PO) & Return to Vendor (RTV)

  • Registered Pharmaceutical Distributors: Directory of major pharmaceutical distributors (GSK, Getz, Abbott, Sanofi, Novartis, Haleon).
  • Purchase Order (PO) Workflow: Create POs, receive stock into inventory batches, and flag payment status (PAID vs DEBT_OWING).
  • Return to Vendor (RTV) Debit Notes: Generate return debit notes for near-expiry or damaged batches with automatic stock deduction and supplier balance credit offset.
  • Supplier Ledger Modal: Printable supplier account statement with purchase logs and debit note reconciliations.

⚙️ 10. Settings, Profile Management, Data Backup & Disaster Recovery

  • Store Profile Customization: Configurable trade name, physical address, phone numbers, NTN, STRN, GSTIN, Drug License Form 20 & 21.
  • Automatic Legacy Data Purger: Automatically cleanses cached legacy mock profiles from browser storage upon initial load.
  • Clear All Profile Fields: One-click manual reset button to clear all profile fields for new testers.
  • Digital Signature PNG Center: Upload PNG signature images with live preview and base64 persistence for automatic embedding on all printed documents.
  • Global Tax Toggles: Enable or disable 18% Sales Tax and 0.5% Advance Tax system-wide.
  • One-Click JSON System Backup & Restore: Export and restore the entire system database in a single JSON backup file (pharmalink_system_backup_YYYY-MM-DD.json).
